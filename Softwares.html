1. Nexus App

ERP and Operations Platform for Manufacturing & Export Businesses

LII Nexus is a web-based ERP and business management suite built for a manufacturing and export company. Before it, teams juggled spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups and email chains for production reports, task follow-ups and approvals. LII Nexus brings these into one role-based platform, so every employee sees the work assigned to them and management gets live visibility.

2. Label Sorter

Market-palace Label Shorting

Label Sorter turns a messy multi-page shipping label PDF into a print-ready batch. It crops the blank margin off each label, detects the courier (Delhivery, Shadowfax, Valmo, FedEx, and more), groups labels by courier, and sorts them by SKU within each group. Everything runs locally in the browser, so no files are uploaded anywhere.
